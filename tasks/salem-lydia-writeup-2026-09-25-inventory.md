CONTRADICTIONS, in order of consequence:
1. Complementiser over-use vs L25 "kẽ links verbs; kì/ki starts a new clause" and L24 "clauses are joined by a new sentence". The only contradiction with numbers behind it (31% vs 15.5%). The authors also say "no zero complementiser" (W-3.2-13), so the fault is not too many linkers in embedded clauses but ki emitted where no embedding exists, or kí where kì belongs. The served line is the likelier cause than the Yoruba/Igbo leakage they guess at (W-3.2-2; the prompt already bans Yoruba tí).
2. Tone marks always (W-2-1) vs REGISTER "no tone marks unless the question asks". Gold carries tone 27.4%, annotator-driven (one writer = 62% of toned answers; "mark all tones" prompts get 32% compliance), Bible 0 of 30,907. The authors' rule would score 72.6% of gold as wrong and cannot be learned from the gold. Keep REGISTER; add the monosyllable tone line (ship item 2).
3. Expanded forms (W-2-2, W-2-4) vs L21 Elision ("drop the FIRST vowel, apostrophe at the joint (w'ọla, k'ọla...)") and REGISTER "apostrophized elision". k'ọla is literally the prompt's model example and the writeup's example of what to avoid. Gold contracts 32.4%; the same annotators write both forms of one phrase; corpus fuses 100%. Scholarship alone cannot overturn this, but L21 reads as obligatory and the data says optional: v4.5 should say "may elide" and keep "never add or strip a word-initial vowel" (the half of W-2-5 that is A-grade and already served).
4. Presentative yì (W-3.1-6, 3.1-8, 3.1-9, 3.1-17) vs L20 "never yí for 'the' (yí = this)" and L18 "de = is at / here is". yi has 0 tokens in 30,907 verses; de 3,762 ("Omi de" = here I am); community yí = this (2 annotators, row 3b5fbaa6). Without tone the model cannot tell yì from yí, so lifting the ban would reopen the determiner error. Scholarship only; not seeded (E-96-11); ask.
5. gwùgwú (W-3.1-4, 3.1-5): 0 corpus hits (tasks/grammar-evidence-scholarship.md conflicts registry #4), now cited a second time by the same author; still one class, still not seeded (E-94-12).
6. Hyphens: àma-ọma, u-gba-ọla-tẹ-ànẹ, gba-àlù (W-3.1-7, 3.1-13, 3.1-14, 3.2-15) vs L26 "no hyphenated prefixes" and rows aa0941af / 4c7b83ae "never with a hyphen, write as one word". Corpus amoma 1,208, community am'onobulẹ / ami imọtọ. Linguist notation, not orthography; prompt wins (same verdict as jwal inventory section 3.3).
7. ìpọ́lú 'Paul' (W-3.2-16) vs L9 "Names ... keep the exact letters ... never respell one" (v4.2 PR #58, annotator-driven). The writeup's own datum respells a name; Bible corpus also respells (Iso, Ebraham). Community wins for the platform; ask whether (7) is data or a recommendation.
8. òmi ì gbọ́ pronoun doubling (W-3.2-18) vs row 95132451 "never both in one slot" and Ejeba 2023's own complementary-distribution claim (E-91-7). Intra-author disagreement.
9. Negator ń and SCL.NEG i̋ (W-3.2-5, 3.2-14, 3.2-17) vs L19 "ONLY a clause-final nasal, written ñ". Segmentally the same system; the acute-n spelling and a tonal negative subject clitic are new.
10. 'his/her' nwu as a free word (W-3.2-8) vs served possessive -wn (L17) and Bible -wñ.
11. nw ~ ñw as free variants (W-1-7) vs L21 "'to/for' is ñwu ... never nwi or plain nw" (allowlist L33 accepts both).
12. Ogwugwu l/r (W-1-10) vs row 003f68fc "buy = la (never ra)" and NEVER WRITE "ra": a dialect-blind ban that would flag an Ogwugwu speaker. Also: is the dialect list's "ogugu" the writeup's "Ogwugwu"?
13. [ŋ] never word-initial (W-1-2) vs served word-initial ñwu / ñw' (L21) and ñyọ (numbers row). Holds only if scoped to plain ñ + vowel.
14. No CVV in a word (W-1-19) vs served kpai (L24), community aidẹ, lia.
15. VC ill-formed (W-1-3) vs the attached negator (-n, 'ñ, ìlì-n) the community writes; the served piece is only "never end a word in -wñ".

QUESTIONS FOR SALEM AND LYDIA:
1. Which variety is "Central / main dialect"? Idah (general_idah, 479 gold), Dekina (Ejeba 2023's data), or Ankpa? Should the model default to one, and which?
2. gwùgwú 'sit.SG': 0 hits in 30,907 Bible verses. Dekina-only? Register-bound? What does an Idah or Ankpa speaker say for (1)?
3. Is clause-final yì (FACT) the same item as the community's yí 'this'? How does "Ùchà lẹ́ tẹ́ yì" relate to "ùchà lẹ́ de"? Would you accept de for 'here is' in community register?
4. Tone: 72.6% of gold is untoned and one annotator writes 62% of all toned answers. Do you want full toning as the gold standard (which means re-annotating 1,000+ rows) or "tone the meaning-bearing monosyllables (kí/kì, jọ/jọ̀, dù)" as the standard?
5. Contraction: should the platform score k'ọla as wrong when 32.4% of gold contracts and the same annotators write both? Or is the furigana layer the ask and gold stays as written?
6. Can three annotators judge (9a) vs (9b) and (10a) vs (10b) blind, so the factive/non-factive kí/kì split gets a community leg before it is served?
7. Dialect mixing (section 4): which outputs, which pairs? Lexical (Uñ vs Ẹnwu), phonological (l/r), orthographic? Without examples nothing can be gated, and the gold itself mixes general_idah and ankpa.
8. Register (section 5): which words were too strong and which child-register phrases broke adult contexts? The vocatives row already distinguishes child vs elder address; we need the failing outputs.
9. ìpọ́lú: elicited data or a recommendation? The names rule came from annotators.
10. Example (6) is cut off after "PL-chi"; please send the full gloss and translation. Prose du/ko vs example dù/kó: which tones to copy?
11. (7) òmi ì gbọ́ has a free pronoun and a clitic together; the 2023 paper says they never co-occur in one slot. Which holds?
12. Negator: ń or ñ? Is i̋ (SCL.NEG) a real tonal negative clitic in Idah, and should it be written?
13. 'his/her' genitive: nwu as a separate word (writeup), -wn (annotators), or -wñ (Bible)?
14. Does "[ŋ] never word-initial" exclude ñw (ñwu 'to/for') and ñy (ñyọ)? Does "no CVV" cover kpai, aidẹ, lia?
15. èmí 'here': confirm it. The model has been inventing mímí and efí for 'here' (both on the NEVER WRITE list); a confirmed èmí closes that hole and lifts W-3.1-17 to B.
16. Ogwugwu: if a question is in Ogwugwu, is ra 'buy' correct? The platform bans ra as Yoruba.
17. Audio: format, length, consent and storage for annotator recordings; is Wikitongues' existing archive the home, or a new table on the gold row?
