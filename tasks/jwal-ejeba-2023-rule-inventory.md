## 3. Contradictions and tensions (single-sourced: flagged, not changed)

1. 3sg object clitic (E-92-9, E-92-15, E-94-7): the served pronoun line fixes 'him/her' as u; Ejeba's Table 5 gives an archiphoneme U that harmonises with the preceding vowel (du ú, but ñà ọ́ 'show him'). Scholarship only, grade C. The pronoun row states the harmony as Ejeba's Dekina data; the prompt line is unchanged.
2. Verb chaining (E-96-5, E-99-11): the served line 'Verbs chain with kẹ: V kẹ V' could lead the model to write kẹ inside du ... wá or du ... tẹ́. Splitting verbs and modifying verbs take no linker. The bring and keep rows say so explicitly.
3. Hyphens (E-90-11, E-95-10): Ejeba hyphenates the plural prefix (àma-) and the t-/r- prefix (tí-nyộ); the corpus and the community write one word (amoma, tinyo) or an elided form (am'onobulẹ). Rows follow the corpus and the community; the paper's hyphen is notation.
4. Global subject clitic V (E-92-16, E-93-6): a subject NP is copied by a vowel clitic in second position; the served small-word gate ('if you cannot say what a particle is doing, remove it') could strip it. Scholarship only, no corpus count yet. Not seeded; listed here so the next failure mine looks for it.
5. Presentative yì (E-96-11) against the served 'de = is at / here is'. Not seeded.
6. gwùgwú 'sit' (E-94-12): 0 hits in 30,907 verses. Not seeded.
7. [ŋm] (E-86-9): the prompt allowlist carries ñm from the community orthography; Ejeba's orthography has no such grapheme. Divergence, not a ban. Note only.
