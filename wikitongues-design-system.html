The Wikitongues AI design system

Everything needed to make something that looks like Wikitongues AI: color, type, spacing, components, the data-viz language, voice, and ready-to-use templates for slide decks and Instagram. Built so a person or a bot can pick it up and stay on brand.

Personality. Warm, humanist, community-first. Accent. #e0a21f ochre. Type. Fraunces · Inter · JetBrains Mono.

01 · Foundations

What this brand is

Wikitongues AI is a collaboration with Wikitongues to teach AI the world's underserved languages, community by community, starting with Igala. It is two things at once: a tutor people practice with, and the public instrument that holds every model accountable. The design has to feel like a living archive made by a community, not a sterile dev tool.

Personality
  • Warm, not corporate. Paper and ink, not chrome.
  • Humanist: the serif headline carries the human voice.
  • Community-first: the people and the language are the subject; the software gets out of the way.
  • Rigorous but honest: it shows uncertainty (an ns cell, a low-confidence flag) instead of faking confidence.
  • Quietly editorial: restraint, generous space, one bold accent.
Wordmark

"Wikitongues" in Fraunces 600, "AI" in the ochre accent text color. Keep at least the cap-height of the "W" as clear space on all sides. Never re-color "Wikitongues", never stretch, never add a drop shadow or outline.

02 · Color

Warm ochre on warm ink

The strategy is restrained: warm-neutral surfaces, ink text, and a single saturated ochre that carries the whole brand. Ochre is the only color that says "Wikitongues AI"; everything else supports it. Body backgrounds are a warm off-white (#fbf8f2), never pure white, and ink is a warm near-black, never #000.

Ochre — the signature accent

#e0a21f (ochre-400) is the brand accent on dark/contrast surfaces. For ochre as text on white, use ochre-600 (#9c6a13) — it clears AA. Never set ochre-400 as small text on white; it fails contrast.

Ink — warm neutral ramp

Supporting hues

Clay, sage, and indigo appear only in data visualization and the pairwise A/B language. They are never used as UI accents.

Semantic tokens

Always design against these role tokens, not raw ramp values. They flip automatically in dark mode.

Contrast rules. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, placeholders ≥ 4.5:1. On any ochre fill, text is ink-950 (#1a1714), never white. On ink surfaces, ochre-300 is the readable accent. When in doubt, push body text toward the ink end of the ramp.

03 · Typography

Three voices, one system

A humanist serif for headlines (the human voice), a clean grotesque for everything functional, and a mono that does real work: it renders Igala tone diacritics cleanly and carries all code and data. Cap the families at these three.

Display
Fraunces

Headlines, hero, pull quotes, big numbers. Weights 400–600. Italic for the one accent word. Soft, "old-style" warmth — the human in the system.

Sans
Inter

Body, UI, labels, buttons, navigation. Weights 400–700. Neutral and legible so the serif and the language stay the stars.

Mono
JetBrains

Igala text (ẹ̀nẹ́), token names, prompt IDs, metrics, code. ss01 on. Mono is functional here, never decorative "techie" styling.

Type scale

Modular, ~1.25–1.33 between steps. Headings use fluid clamp(). Display ceiling 4.5rem; letter-spacing floor −0.025em.

Rules. Headings: Fraunces 500, letter-spacing −0.01em (tighter on hero). Body line length 65–75ch, line-height 1.55 (1.6 for large body). No all-caps sentences; reserve uppercase for ≤4-word labels. Igala always renders in the mono slot so tone marks stay crisp.

Shadows are warm and low-contrast (tinted with ink, not black). Most surfaces sit on sm; only menus/modals/toasts go higher.

Motion
  • Default transition: 0.18s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) — a gentle ease-out.
  • Ease out, never bounce or elastic.
  • Animate transform/opacity; avoid animating layout.
  • Focus ring: 0 0 0 3px ochre-200.
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion: collapse to a near-instant crossfade.

06 · Data visualization

The arena language

The leaderboard and charts use a fixed, colorblind-aware system. Two rules: the A/B pair is always indigo vs clay (never two reds/greens), and rubric strength uses a diverging red → ochre → green ramp at low opacity so the number, not the color, leads.

Pairwise A / B / tie
A · #3f5a8a indigo B · #b5512f clay tie · ink-500
Score ramp (Bradley-Terry strength)

lo #b23b32 → mid #b5790f → hi #2f7d54, filled at ~16% opacity behind the number. An ns cell (not statistically distinguishable) shows the value in muted gray with an ns tag — honesty over false precision.

07 · Voice & copy

How it sounds

Factual, direct, down to earth, humble. It explains hard ideas plainly and admits what it does not know. It never sells. The language and the community are the subject; the technology is the means.

Do
  • Name the specific thing: "tone marks were dropped," not "quality issues."
  • Admit uncertainty: "this is an early pilot, it will make mistakes."
  • Credit the community as the teacher.
  • Verb + object on buttons: "Start practicing," "Flag prompt."
  • Plain sentences. Short where it helps, not as a tic.
Don't
  • No em dashes. Use commas, colons, or periods.
  • No buzzwords: streamline, empower, seamless, world-class, transform.
  • No hype or false confidence about the model's ability.
  • No ALL-CAPS sentences; caps only for short labels.
  • No aphoristic "serious statement, then punchy negation" rhythm.
Voice in one line

"Today's AI models barely speak Igala, and what they do produce is often wrong. The point of this project is to change that, and to build the first public benchmark that holds every model accountable."

08 · Imagery

Photography & texture

Wikitongues' real archive is people speaking to camera, in their own places. Lean on that: documentary portraits of speakers and communities, warm natural light, real settings. Avoid generic "AI/tech" stock, glowing brains, circuit boards, and blue gradients.

Subject
  • Real speakers and community members, named in alt text.
  • Hands writing, markets, gatherings, place.
  • Text-as-texture: Igala words set large in Fraunces/mono.
Treatment
  • Warm, natural color; or a duotone in ink + ochre.
  • No heavy filters, no blue tech wash.
  • Full-bleed when the photo can carry the fold.

Ink → ochre duotone target

When there's no photo
  • Set an Igala word or proverb large as the hero element.
  • Use the ochre drench or ink field as the canvas.
  • Never a flat gray block where an image belongs.
